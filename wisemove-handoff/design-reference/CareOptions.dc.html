Assisted Living

A private apartment with help available around the clock for daily activities, plus meals, housekeeping, transportation and a full calendar of social events.

Often a good fit when someone:
  • Needs reminders or help with medications, bathing or dressing
  • Is lonely or isolated at home
  • Can no longer manage cooking, cleaning or driving safely

Memory Care

A secure setting designed for people living with Alzheimer's or other dementias, with specially trained staff, calming design and structured daily activities.

Often a good fit when someone:
  • Has a dementia diagnosis and needs more supervision
  • Wanders, gets lost, or is unsafe alone
  • Shows confusion, agitation or sundowning that's hard to manage at home

Long-Term Care (Skilled Nursing)

24-hour care from licensed nurses for people with complex medical needs, limited mobility, or recovery that requires ongoing clinical support.

Often a good fit when someone:
  • Needs daily medical care or two-person assistance
  • Is being discharged from a hospital or rehab
  • Has a condition that needs frequent nursing attention
May be covered in part by Medicaid · We can explain your options
