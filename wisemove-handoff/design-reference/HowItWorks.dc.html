How it works

A clear path through a hard decision

Most families have never done this before. We have, many times. Here's exactly what happens when you work with WiseMove, and it never costs you a cent.

Schedule a free consultation
1

Free consultation

A 30–45 minute conversation by phone, video or in person. We learn about health and care needs, daily routines, budget and benefits, preferred location, and the things that make your loved one feel at home.

Have ready: medication list, recent doctor notes, a rough monthly budget
2

Your personal shortlist

Within [2–3 days] we send a short list of communities that fit, with honest notes on pricing, care levels, staffing, recent inspection results and current availability. No endless online searching.

3

Guided tours

We book the tours, plan an efficient route, and join you. We know what to look for, from how staff talk to residents to what's really included in the monthly fee.

Download our tour checklist →
4

Decision & move-in support

We help compare options, review agreements, coordinate assessments and paperwork, and connect you with trusted movers and downsizing help.

5

We check in afterward

After move-in we follow up to make sure your loved one is settling in well, and we're here if needs change later.

Why is WiseMove free?

Partner communities pay us a referral fee only when a family chooses them and moves in. That fee comes out of their marketing budget, never added to your rent.

Common questions

How long does the process take?

It depends on urgency. We can help with a hospital discharge in days, or take months if you're planning ahead.

Do I have to choose one of your suggestions?

No. You're never obligated. Our goal is to help you make a confident choice.

Can you help if my parent lives in another city?

Yes. Many families we help live out of town. We can tour on your behalf and share video and notes.
